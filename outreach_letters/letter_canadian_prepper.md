Here is how it works: you install local AI models on your own machines — your desktop, your laptop, an old computer in the garage, a Raspberry Pi cluster if you have one. A stronger local AI model acts as a coordinator. You give it a task — anything from writing a document to analyzing data to planning logistics — and it breaks the task into independent pieces, sends each piece to a different machine on your local network, each machine processes its piece completely independently with zero communication between them, and the coordinator puts the results back together. The whole thing runs inside your house. Nothing leaves. Nothing.
