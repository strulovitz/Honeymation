How it works: a local AI model acts as an automated coordinator. It takes a task — analyzing pathology reports, reviewing imaging results, drafting treatment summaries, processing clinical trial eligibility — breaks it into independent sub-tasks, and distributes each one to a separate computer on the hospital's internal network. Each computer processes its sub-task independently. When done, the coordinator combines the results. The entire pipeline runs behind the hospital's firewall.
