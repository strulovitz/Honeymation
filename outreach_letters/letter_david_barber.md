Think of how Britain has always operated at its best. Greenwich did not put a clock in every ship — it set the STANDARD that every ship's clock synchronized to. The architecture scales through hierarchy: a top-level coordinator gives sub-problems to lower-level coordinators, who decompose further and distribute to their own workers. Intelligence cascades downward through layers. Results flow back up. No single node holds the full picture. The capability grows through architecture, not by making any individual machine more powerful. Your centre provides the foundational models and standards. My system distributes the execution across the country without any data leaving each local site.
