It takes a task — radiology reports to analyze, discharge summaries to draft, patient notes to review, clinical data to process — breaks it into independent sub-tasks, and distributes each one to a separate computer on the hospital's internal network. Each computer processes its sub-task independently. When done, the coordinator combines the results. The entire pipeline runs behind Bellevue's firewall.
