I am a software developer and independent inventor. I built an open-source distributed AI system that runs entirely on your own computers, on your own local network, with zero cloud dependency. No data ever leaves your network. No API calls. No telemetry. No corporate servers. Nothing phoning home. You could air-gap it and it would still function.

How it works: you install open-weight local AI models on your own machines. A stronger local model acts as a coordinator. You give it any task and it breaks it into independent pieces, distributes each piece to a different machine on your local network, each machine processes its piece with its own AI model completely independently, and the coordinator combines the results. Everything stays behind your firewall. Big Tech gets nothing — not your prompts, not your data, not even the knowledge that you are running it.
