## Full Letter

To whom it may concern — and it should concern you greatly,

Sun Tzu wrote that the greatest victory is turning an enemy into an ally. I am writing to you as an enemy who would prefer to be your ally. But the window for that is closing.

My name is Nir Strulovitz. I am a software developer, a lawyer (bar-licensed), and an independent inventor. I built a fully functional, open-source, distributed AI system that does the following: it takes any task, decomposes it into independent subtasks, distributes each subtask to a separate machine running its own complete large language model, executes all subtasks in parallel with zero communication between worker machines, and combines the results. No data ever leaves the building. Not a single byte. No internet connection required. No subscription. No license fee. No vendor lock-in. Free. Forever.

The system scales infinitely through hierarchical "hives" — a coordinator delegates to sub-coordinators, who delegate to their own workers. Add more levels, add more machines, handle problems of unlimited complexity. This is not theoretical. This is working software. It is on GitHub right now. It has been demonstrated, documented, and explained in two animated videos and a full-length book.

I want to be direct with you about what this means for your business.

**The Precedents You Should Study:**

Because a rogue player who disrupts your business once will disrupt it again. Paying him a salary to prevent the next disruption was the smartest business decision OpenAI made that year. The software was free. The insurance policy was not.

Matt Schlicht created Moltbook. Meta bought him almost immediately. Again — they didn't need the software. It was free. They needed to prevent the NEXT thing he would build. For Meta, his acquisition price was less than their budget for office snacks. For the prevention of a potential catastrophic competitive threat, it was priceless.

That is approximately 90% of what your paying customers use you for. Maybe 95%.
