## Key angle: Inter-hive privacy
- France deploys Queen Bees in hospitals and military bases across the country
- Each site = independent hive, private by architecture internally
- For resilience: hives must connect (if one Queen goes down, another takes over)
- THOSE connections leak information: what tasks, what results, what capacity
- Her metric privacy / QIF / geo-indistinguishability = the tool to protect inter-hive comms
- "Geo-indistinguishability protects position in physical space. Could a similar framework protect position in informational space?"
